SASSI-EDU · guided course

Explainer videos

From fixed-base seismic design to soil-structure interaction with SASSI, in eleven short narrated explainers, one per lesson. Each one gives you the big idea of its lesson in plain words, with the numbers and curves of the lesson's own runs; the lesson itself is where you run the commands.

Start with lesson 1 Short, plain-language explainers for newcomers, 3 to 4 minutes each, narrated with captions and a transcript. Change the voice or the speed in the player (⚙).
Keyboard in the player: space play / pause, ← → previous / next sentence, shift ← → chapters, c captions, m voice, t transcript, f full screen. SASSI-EDU is an educational re-implementation of the SASSI methodology and is not affiliated with the developers of ACS SASSI.
